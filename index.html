Product Notes

Advanced Performance Settings

Visual settings panel: Tune frame caps, resolution scale, particle limits, noise resolution, starfield density, gas giant detail, ring geometry, and lighting from one place.

Gas Giant Planet Type

Layered atmospheres: Build gas giants with configurable strata count, color bands, scale, warp, noise, and atmospheric depth.

Advanced Water Rendering

Ocean shader: Animated waves, light refraction, Fresnel response, and depth-aware transparency now work together for cleaner water surfaces.

Independent Atmosphere Controls

Separated controls: Atmosphere opacity and cloud opacity can now be balanced independently for subtler or more dramatic planetary profiles.

Enhanced Mobile Experience

Mobile focus system: Touch controls now provide faster access to planets, moons, and stars with a dedicated focus control for smaller screens.

Advanced Physics & Collisions

Orbital interactions: Moon physics, collision detection, impact deformation, crater generation, and two-way gravity now produce more dynamic systems.
